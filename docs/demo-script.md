# Royal Square Financial Hackathon Demo Script

Follow this step-by-step 19-step demo script to showcase the unified solution:

1. **Step 1 - Client Login**: Sign in as `sipho.dlamini@royalsquare.co.za` (Demo client).
2. **Step 2 - Financial Position**: Inspect Net Worth (R 4,850,000.00), breakdown of Assets (Property, Vehicle), Liabilities (Bond, Vehicle Finance), and Investments (Allan Gray Balanced Fund).
3. **Step 3 - Financial Goals**: View Active Goals (Retirement 2035 at 65% completion, Children's Education at 45%).
4. **Step 4 - Create Service Request**: Tap "Make a Request" -> Select "Policy Document" -> Request updated schedule for Discovery Life.
5. **Step 5 - Request Confirmation**: Verify request `RSF-20250919-...` appears immediately with status "Submitted" and step 1 checked.
6. **Step 6 - Open Adviser Dashboard**: Switch to Royal Square Adviser view (or login as `adviser@royalsquare.co.za`).
7. **Step 7 - Adviser Inbox**: See new request badge and unread notification in top bar.
8. **Step 8 - Open Request**: Click on Sipho Dlamini's Policy Document request.
9. **Step 9 - Assign & Process**: Assign to adviser, mark status as "In Progress", add note "Requested copy from Discovery portal".
10. **Step 10 - Advance Workflow**: Complete workflow step 3 and upload generated document.
11. **Step 11 - Return to Client View**: Switch back to Client PWA.
12. **Step 12 - View Real-time Update**: See instant notification and request progress moving to "Completed".
13. **Step 13 - Accident Workflow**: Tap "Report Accident / Loss".
14. **Step 14 - Motor Claim Submission**: Fill incident time, Sandton location, rear collision description, police case number `CAS 421/09/2025`, and select Santam.
15. **Step 15 - Claim in Dashboard**: Open Adviser Dashboard -> Claims -> View `CLM-...` in "Reported" status.
16. **Step 16 - Simulate Insurer Stages**: Trigger mock insurer webhook -> Status transitions: "Handler Assigned: Sarah Van Der Merwe", "Assessment Complete", "Repair Authorised".
17. **Step 17 - Client Timeline**: Client mobile view reflects updated live timeline with Santam handler phone/email and panel beater booking.
18. **Step 18 - Tasks & Reminders**: Show Driver's Licence expiry reminder and Annual Review scheduled.
19. **Step 19 - Audit Trail**: Adviser inspects compliance audit logs verifying immutable timestamps, IP, and user operations.
